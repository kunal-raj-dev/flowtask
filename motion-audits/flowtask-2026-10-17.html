02 / Through Emil’s lens

Working well: the logo is still, task rows do not stagger into view and keyboard task scrolling is immediate.

03 / Through Jakub’s lens

03 · IMPORTANT · RESOLVED

Reduced motion had two sources of truth.

Native View Transitions checked the operating system preference but ignored FlowTask’s own reduced-motion setting. The wrapper now checks both before taking a snapshot; the direct update remains available when motion is disabled or unsupported.

Working well: the CSS preference guards cover descendants and pseudo-elements; immersive video has a still fallback. Native capabilities degrade to in-app controls.

Through Jakub’s lens: consistency across settings matters more than adding another transition.
